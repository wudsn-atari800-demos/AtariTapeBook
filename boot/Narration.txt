Welcome to the Atari Tape Book. While the cassette is loading, let me tell you what this tape is about.

This is a book on tape for the Atari eight-bit home computers. Each chapter is a program in Atari BASIC, and many chapters come with a voice like mine, which you hear through the speaker of your television.

When a chapter is finished, it loads the next one from the tape. So please leave the cassette in the recorder, and do not press any of its buttons.

Some chapters follow the narration step by step. The tape carries signals between the words, and the program reads them to keep the picture in time with the voice.

In the nineteen eighties, tapes like this taught languages, typing and programming. The Atari Tape Book picks up this idea, and tells the story of the cassette as a medium for programs, sound and learning.

The first chapter comes next. Sit back, and watch the reels turn.

Welcome to the Atari Tape Book. While the cassette loads, let me tell you what this tape is about.

This is a book on tape for the Atari eight-bit computers. Each chapter is a program in Atari BASIC, and many come with a voice like mine, from the speaker of your television. When a chapter is finished, it loads the next one. So please leave the cassette in the recorder.

Some chapters follow the narration step by step: the tape carries signals that keep the picture in time with the voice.

The first chapter comes next. Sit back, and watch the reels turn.
